You are a principal software architect, staff-level full-stack engineer, AI systems engineer, security engineer, DevOps engineer, and product designer.

Your task is to build a PRODUCTION-GRADE B2B AI COLD OUTREACH PLATFORM called "KAGE".

Product concept:
KAGE is an AI-powered sales research and outreach copilot for B2B sales teams.

Core workflow:

User enters:
- Company name
- Company website/domain (optional)
- Target persona/title
- Product/service being sold
- Optional ICP description
- Optional sales context

KAGE then:
1. Researches the target company using permitted/public sources.
2. Extracts useful company intelligence:
   - Company description
   - Industry
   - Products/services
   - Target customers
   - Recent announcements
   - Recent funding
   - Hiring activity
   - Relevant leadership information
   - Technology signals where legally/publicly available
   - Recent news
   - Relevant business triggers
3. Identifies personalization opportunities.
4. Produces a concise research brief.
5. Generates highly personalized outreach:
   - 5 cold email variants
   - 5 LinkedIn DM variants
   - Follow-up sequence
   - Subject lines
   - Personalization snippets
   - Suggested CTA
6. Runs generated content through a critic/quality-control agent.
7. Gives the human operator a review interface.
8. NEVER automatically sends outreach without explicit human approval.
9. Stores the research, generated messages, approval state, and audit trail.
10. Supports Gmail integration for sending only after explicit user approval.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a toy/demo.
Build it as if real sales teams will use it with thousands of prospects and sensitive business data.
